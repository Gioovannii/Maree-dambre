# Décor des ressources — baie compacte

Créé avec l’outil intégré ImageGen, à partir de RessourcesPortrait et CentreVillePortrait.
Fichier utilisé : `MareesAmbre/Assets.xcassets/RessourcesBaie.imageset/RessourcesBaie.png`.

## Prompt initial

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a replacement background for an iPhone strategy game resource district. Image 1 is the old elongated resource map to redesign; image 2 is the town center STYLE reference. Match image 2's charming detailed sunlit 2.5D Mediterranean miniature, rocks, foliage, warm paths and turquoise sea. Portrait 9:19.5 canvas. Crucial: COMPACT BROAD ROUND island plateau around a small CENTRAL turquoise bay and tiny pier, not a long thin peninsula. All ten resource sites clustered in a roughly square area between y=0.25 and y=0.73, with generous calm scenery above and sea below for UI. Ten distinct resource clearings, no labels: top row forest at x=.22 y=.26, farm x=.50 y=.26, amber quarry x=.78 y=.26; second row forest x=.20 y=.42, farm x=.80 y=.42; third row forest x=.20 y=.57, amber quarry x=.80 y=.57; bottom row farm x=.22 y=.72, forest x=.50 y=.72, amber quarry x=.78 y=.72. Small resource props border each sandy clearing, centers remain open for level badges. Central bay pier at x=.50 y=.45. No central city or large building. Equal scale and close overhead camera like town reference. Rich natural winding footpaths link ten clearings. No text, no UI, no borders, no symbols. Preserve complete clearings inside frame. Landscape occupies full canvas naturally.

## Retouches finales

Convertir la forêt du bas au centre en cultures maraîchères et la carrière du bas à droite en champ de blé. Convertir uniquement la clairière du bas à gauche en champ de blé, en conservant les trois forêts au-dessus. Conserver les positions, la baie, les sentiers et le style. Le décor final comporte trois forêts, cinq champs et deux carrières, correspondant aux dix zones existantes.
