# TestFlight candidate — 1.0 (21)

## Changes

- Incoming automatic raids target one village across the domain, with variable intervals of 8–15 days plus nightly postponement when needed. The announced target persists through reloads, village switching and colony arrivals.
- Missions now guide established domains from their second settlement toward colony development, stronger weapons and preparation for a third settlement.
- People selection identifies transport, defensive and fast-raiding play styles. The defensive people is recommended for beginners; Missions includes tactical advice. The old human wood and elven amber production bonuses are removed.
- Restoring a backup displays local and incoming progression before confirmation, including export date, settlements, buildings, fields and cumulative levels.
- New exports are authenticated using a Keychain-held key. Modified files, unsigned JSON and older unsigned exports are rejected through both file and iCloud restore paths. Existing local games remain playable.
- iPad Missions use a full page with the village header above one scrolling area.

## Important transfer change

Export or upload a new protected backup from the device retaining your game. Old unsigned backups cannot be restored. Cross-device transfers require the export’s authentication key to be available through iCloud Keychain. Keep the original game until restoration succeeds. Authentication protects transferred files, not the truth of client-generated progression.

## Device validation

Follow [Next beta checks](NEXT-BETA-CHECKS.md). Real Keychain synchronization between two signed devices still needs verification. A local archive is not an App Store Connect upload or a completed Apple distribution validation.
