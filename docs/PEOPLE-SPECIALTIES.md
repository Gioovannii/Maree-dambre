# First playable people specialties

Existing production bonuses remain. This first increment specializes existing
buildings; it does not add exclusive building art, a prepared spell, or new units.

- Humans: Market adds five carrying bonus points per level, capped at twenty.
  Raids, trading escorts and internal shipments snapshot transport at departure.
- Reef folk: Watchtower adds five defense points per level beyond its normal
  defense. The bonus remains in the settlement when troops leave.
- Marsh elves: Academy reduces only the distance surcharge of raids by two
  percentage points per level, capped at twenty percent. Base travel and nearby
  home targets remain unchanged; total duration rounds up to whole seconds.

Missions displays the specialty, its rules and whether its building is present.
Each settlement unlocks its specialty independently. No new saved fields are
required. Next increments: faction-specific building presentation, prepared
reef protection and enemy reconnaissance for elves.
