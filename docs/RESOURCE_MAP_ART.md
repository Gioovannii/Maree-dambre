# Resource domain artwork

Historical empty-clearing iteration. The current app uses the integrated fixed map described in [RESOURCE_MAP_FIXED_ART.md](RESOURCE_MAP_FIXED_ART.md).

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, 2026-10-15. Reference images: `ChampsDomaine.png` (palette) and `ParcelleBois.png` (sprite perspective and lighting). The original background is preserved; the game now uses `ChampsDomaineClairieres.imageset`.

SwiftUI supplies all interactive resource sprites, selection, levels and upgrade timers. The painted clearings are scenery. Coordinates in `VillageMapMode.mapPoint(for:)` follow the delivered artwork rather than assuming that the requested coordinates were reproduced exactly. Plot identifiers, resource types and saved progress retain their original meaning.

## Generation prompt

Create a production game BACKGROUND asset, portrait aspect 1:2, for a Mediterranean coastal resource domain in an isometric strategy game. Use image 1 only as palette and art direction reference and image 2 to MATCH camera angle, lighting and ground texture of our interactive sprites. The current reference is far too cluttered. Redesign from scratch, mature polished hand-painted strategy game art with clearly readable land use, coherent perspective, sunlight from top left, olive greens, muted earth, warm stone, turquoise sea. NO UI, text, numbers, badges, white outlines, buildings, floating tiles or drawn resource sprites. This is a terrain layer onto which 10 resource sprites will be placed. Broad continuous gently sloping grassy mainland filling x=10%-90%, y=22%-80%. Narrow rocky coastline and calm sea along upper 18% and bottom 15%; background Mediterranean hills only at extreme edges. Most playable land must be uncluttered softly textured grass and earth, much less texture than reference. Winding sandy paths connect 10 CLEAR SMALL OPEN FLAT EARTH/GRASS CLEARINGS with approximate center normalized x/y: (0.25,0.30), (0.53,0.29), (0.77,0.38), (0.28,0.43), (0.75,0.51), (0.21,0.56), (0.48,0.55), (0.74,0.64), (0.36,0.68), (0.56,0.75). Paths weave BETWEEN clearings, do not cross their centers. Each clearing fits an isometric diamond footprint 20% canvas width, and MUST stay free of bushes, trees, rocks, structures. Clearings blend into continuous terrain, absolutely NO separate square platforms or visible tile grid. Sparse dark olive woods behind upper-left clearings at perimeter only; sparse warm exposed rocks at right edge; low terraced pasture in lower area. A small rustic bridge crosses a narrow stream at y=81%; path continues toward a modest coastal gateway at y=86% where a town-navigation button will overlay. Designed spatially as a believable connected estate, asymmetrical yet organized, large breathing spaces. Top 15% and bottom 10% visually quiet for UI overlays. High quality game background, consistent scale, no excessive flowers, no photorealism, no blur.

## Design choices and checks

Native SwiftUI, iPhone and iPad, existing amber/sea palette. Design variance 6/10, motion 1/10, density 4/10. Asymmetric placement follows the paths; only the selected plot has an amber ground outline. Small numbered medallions show levels without repeated labels or chevrons; resource names and production remain in the detail panel. Existing VoiceOver descriptions and the large-text list presentation remain available. No new decorative animation.

Check the resource screenshots on both devices, the compact-screen spacing assertions in `check-layout-snapshot.sh`, the packaged French/English strings, and the Xcode build after changing this asset or its anchors. Screenshot inspection verifies layout, not comprehension by new players; a player usability test is still needed.
